# threejs-water-optics — not ported

This is a stub. The web starter kit's skill of this name is written against
three.js shaders, so its content was not copied into the Unreal Starter Kit.
Source: the web starter kit, `agents/skills/threejs-water-optics/`.

**What it covered in the web kit:** water whose waves, normals, reflection,
refraction, absorption and foam all come from one shared surface description, so
the optics agree with the motion.

**What the Unreal equivalent would cover:** water built on the engine's own water
shading and translucency, its surface and interaction driven from one state, and
its look measured and judged in a rendered window under the lab stage's lighting.

**Until it is ported:** `dev-lab-authoring`, `visual-verification-gate`, AGENTS.md
rule 2b (read the numbers — a surface height, a depth — before judging a frame).
